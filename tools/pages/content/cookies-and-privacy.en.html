What personal data do we collect?

We have minimized the collection of personal data through the website, leaving everything in your hands.   • We use the collected data from our contact form only for feedback to you about your inquiry / request / question. We do not provide this data to third parties.

  • By emailing and the name you leave by subscribing to our Newsletter, we send you a notification every time we have a new post on the website.
  • The data you fill out when purchasing a product, webinar or other service that requires payment is used to identify you when providing the product or service in question.

How long do we store your data?

Your data is stored for a period of 1 year and then automatically deleted from the system. The data you provide us with regarding your subscription is only deleted after you opt out of this subscription.

What are my rights as a user?

You have certain rights to your data as a user on this website and you could request them from us. Here are some of them:

Right to data portability

You may transfer your data from one service provider to another. This needs to happen in a format that is understandable for most devices. You may request all of your data stored on this web site.

Right to be forgotten

If users do not wish to visit the Web site anymore and use its services, they have the right, after downloading their data, to completely delete them. You can submit a delete request and our administrators will complete the process. You will then not be able to enter your account.

/You need to be a registered user/

Right to anonymity

You have the right to stay anonymous! Go to your profile and change your settings. Go to your account and make anonymous the data you want to be anonymous.

/You need to be a registered user/

Right to data correction

This ensures that users can adjust their data at any time. Contact us to let us know what you would like to change in your data.

Right to prohibit the use of data

Individuals may request that their data is not used for any additional processes. Information will be retained but will not be used.

Right to be notified

In case of leakage of personal data, the injured parties have the right to be informed within 72 hours of the event.

Cookie Law

The Cookies Act is part of the Online Privacy Act. Web sites are required to gain the consent of their visitors to store or retrieve information from and on their browser.

How do I disable the cookie placement?

All modern browsers let you change your cookie settings. Typically, you can find these settings in the options menu or preferences of your browser. To be aware of these settings, the following links can help you or you can use the Help button from your browser menus for more details:

  • Cookie settings in Internet Explorer
  • Cookie settings in Firefox
  • Cookie settings in Chrome
  • Cookie settings in Safari web and iOS

Please remember that if you choose to stop/deactivate/delete cookies, some sections of our site may not work properly.

For Microsoft Internet Explorer:

  • Tools> Internet Options> Privacy Tab
  • Use the slider to set the options, or use the advanced options (Advanced …)

For Mozilla:

  • Tools> Cookie Manager OR
  • Edit> Preferences> Privacy & Security
  • Set options in the Cookies item
  • Cookie Sites allow domain settings: Block / Session / Allow
  • Stored Cookies open a cookie window that displays details of stored cookies, and can be deleted or blocked

For Opera

  • Opera> Settings> Preferences … OR
  • Ctrl + F12
  • Advanced> Cookies
  • Accept cookies – the use of cookies is allowed. Value is set by default.
  • I accept only cookies from the site I visit – allow the use of cookies only from the site you visited.
  • Never accept cookies – disallowing cookies.
  • Delete new cookies when exiting Opera – delete new cookies after closing the browser. If you’ve visited a new site that saves information in the cookies, after the browser is closed, newly created cookies are automatically deleted.
  • Ask me before accepting cookies – the browser asks the user if they want to / do not use the cookies when needed.
  • Manage Cookies – Organize cookies.

For Google Chrome:

  • Options> Advanced settings
  • Privacy> Content settings> Cookies
  • Allow local data to be set (recommended) – the value is set by default.
  • Allow local data to be set only for the current session.
  • Blocking data from sites completely.
  • Block third-party cookies – yes / no
  • Cookies and other site and plug-in data cleared when I close my browser – yes / no
