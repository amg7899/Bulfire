BULFIRE Ltd. Fire fighting vehicles

  • Specialized fire trucks for extinguishing forest fires

  • Specialized fire tractors for extinguishing forest and field fires

  • General purpose fire trucks

  • Fire trucks transformations

  • Specialized service and installation of lifting fire, and rescue equipment on existing fire trucks

  • Tankers with clearance up to 600+ mm, up to 60.000 L fuel for filling of military vehicles at firing positions

Partners

BULFIRE Ltd. is a Bulgarian company that started operations in 1997 and has been developing active commercial and production activities for over a quarter of a century. The company’s main specialization is the design, production, and trade of fire equipment, which is where the name of the company BULFIRE comes from.

Today, Bulfire is the operator of the Burgas Free Trade Zone.
