# BULFIRE website

Static site (plain HTML + CSS + a little JS, no build step) for BULFIRE Ltd., replacing the old WordPress site at https://bulfire.ecoes.eu/. A sister of ecoes.eu (repo amg7899/SUM, its CLAUDE.md has the full history of the shared design decisions) and franchise.ecoes.eu (repo amg7899/FranchiseEcoes). Started 2026-11-26 at the client's request ("modernise it like the previous 2 sites").

## Publishing
- GitHub Pages from the branch `claude/festive-ride-jemver` (root). Preview: https://amg7899.github.io/Bulfire/ once the client switches Pages on (Settings → Pages → Deploy from a branch → this branch, / (root)); no tool here can.
- All paths are relative, so the site works at the preview address and on the domain.
- Go-live (when the client approves the new site): add a `CNAME` file with `bulfire.ecoes.eu` (or set the custom domain in Settings → Pages, which writes it), and the client asks SuperHosting (registrar/DNS of ecoes.eu, see SUM's CLAUDE.md) for one record: `bulfire` CNAME → `amg7899.github.io` (replacing its current A record). Not before: a CNAME file would send the preview to the old WordPress. E-mail is not affected (addresses are @ecoes.eu). Then Enforce HTTPS. The ecoes.eu footers already link to bulfire.ecoes.eu.
- The old site: WordPress (theme Woodmart, WPBakery, WooCommerce, GTranslate dropdown) at SuperHosting. Reachable from this environment since 2026-11-26 (bulfire.ecoes.eu added to the environment's allowed hosts). SuperHosting's bot check ("Please wait while your request is being verified") blocks repeated page loads in a browser; the REST API works: `wp-json/wp/v2/pages?per_page=100`, `wp-json/wp/v2/media/<id>`, `wp-json/wc/store/v1/products?per_page=100&page=N`, images from `wp-content/uploads/…`. Page content is WPBakery shortcodes with curly quotes (`vc_single_image image=“862″` → media id).

## Design (sister of ecoes.eu, BULFIRE's own colours)
- Kept from ecoes.eu (client-approved there): Jost (display) + Geologica (text), both with Cyrillic; the header (☰ left of the logo, opens every section full-screen; one-line row of links scaled by main.js down to 74%, only ☰ below 1024px; e-mail box gives way first; language button showing the other language), the dropdown with a line dropping to a white panel with diamond markers, white cards with soft corners (`.plate`), pill buttons with a round arrow chip, tiles that lift on hover, the light + transparent landscape in hero, page heads, menu and footer, the quality floor (≥4.5:1 text, 2px focus ring, 44px targets, no horizontal scroll 320–1920px, reduced motion, WebP + JPEG with width/height).
- Palette (tokens in style.css): smoke `#f3f5f6` (page), paper `#fff`, sky `#e2eaf0` (top of the light), glow `#f7e7d3` (warm horizon at the bottom of the light, footer; amber, not pink: pink was tried and looked salmon), ink `#1c2329` (chassis charcoal), text `#3b434a`, muted `#5c656d` (≥4.8:1 on all grounds), line `#e0e5e8`, line-strong `#c8d0d6`, fire `#d3141b` (buttons with white text 5.4:1, drawn lines, nodes, markers, arrow chips; the logo red is `#fc0a03`, too bright for text), fire-deep `#b3131a` (links, focus ring, current page, hover), fire-soft `#fdeceb`.
- Home hero: everything centred: the logo's flame drawn in five contour lines (outer red to amber core, as the logo's layered tongues; paths scaled from one outline around a point inside the flame), then "BULFIRE Ltd." small, the headline "Fire fighting vehicles" (the client's heading of the vehicle tiles) huge in ink, a short red line. The flame stands above the words like the flame rising out of the U in the logo. Tried first: the flame large and faint behind the words, as the globe on ecoes.eu: the flame is tall and narrow, the words hid its body and only stray lines showed. The contours draw in once on load, core first (the only non-user motion; drawn at once under reduced motion). The headline breaks a very long translated word instead of being cut (`overflow-wrap: anywhere`, h1 max-width 100%).
- Transparent landscape: forested hills (conifer treelines) in translucent white with white edges, white only; `assets/img/deco/forest.svg` (page heads, menu, footer mirrored) and `forest-hero.svg` (symmetrical, forest outside the middle 50%, a clearing under the words), drawn by `tools/forest.py` (seeded; stands taper at their ends so the forest thins out instead of ending in a wall). - Tiles: the six images of the old home page (1232×411 collages on white), title below with a red arrow chip; 1 column, 2 from 760px, 3 from 1240px. Titles in sentence case (the old site had capitals).
- Partners (from the old home page's side column): KINTEX, TEREM Holding, Rosenbauer, Jöhstadt, white logo cards linking out (new tab); the files are small (300–350px) JPEGs from the old site.
- Footer: the company paragraph beside the BULFIRE logo; five columns: Company information (BULFIRE Ltd., UIC), Useful links (legal pages), Contact us (address, phone, mobile, e-mail, fax), Email addresses (by department) + Websites, WhatsApp numbers by language (wa.me links); then the main links, © 2026 BULFIRE and the ECOES JV logo (→ ecoes.eu). `<address>` columns are set to normal (not italic) type.

## Languages (English + Bulgarian)
- `bg/` mirrors every English page with the same markup (asset paths one more `../`, links stay inside `bg/`); every change goes to both. English pages carry our Bulgarian nav names in `span.l10n-bg` (shown when the browser translates to Bulgarian); main.js moves a translated page to our `bg/` version (sessionStorage key `bulfire-lang`). Brand names, e-mails, phones and addresses carry `translate="no"` with neighbouring spaces inside the spans.
- The old site had no Bulgarian text (only Google auto-translate), so the Bulgarian is our translation, for the client to review. Terms used: противопожарни автомобили; всъдеходни и високопроходими противопожарни автомобили; сервиз и трансформации на пожарни автомобили (asked: "трансформации" or "преоборудване"?); високопроходими противопожарни трактори; онлайн магазини BULFIRE; Свободна безмитна зона – Бургас; ЕИК for UIC. Company/brand names in Latin (BULFIRE Ltd., FIRE TECHNICS, AGRITRUCK); the head office as on ecoes.eu: бул. „Ал. Стамболийски“ 205, офис 320А, София 1309, България, ЕС. Numbers in Bulgarian style (60&nbsp;000 л, мм).
- Every page's head carries `rel=canonical` and `rel=alternate` hreflang en / bg / x-default with absolute https://bulfire.ecoes.eu/ URLs.
